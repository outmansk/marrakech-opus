**In short:** if you are staying **several months or more**, a **long-term rental** (one-year minimum lease) is almost always the better-value and more stable option. **Short-term** rental suits a stay of a few days or weeks, or a trial run before committing, but it costs more per night and leaves less room for everyday life.

## What really differs between the two

| | Long-term | Short-term |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | One-year minimum lease | A few nights to a few weeks |
| **Monthly cost** | Lower: you pay rent, not a holiday rate | Higher per night |
| **Flexibility** | One-year commitment | Very flexible |
| **Proof of address** | Yes, the written lease | Rarely usable |
| **Property** | Furnished or unfurnished | Almost always furnished and equipped |
| **Paperwork** | Lease, inventory, deposit | Simple booking |

## Long-term rental: settling in for good

This is the choice of expats, families, retirees and remote workers who want to live in Marrakech day to day. The rules we apply at Live In Marrakech are simple:

- **One-year minimum lease**, in writing.
- **Security deposit**: two months' rent for a **furnished** home, one month for an **unfurnished** one.
- **Documents**: a passport or national ID card is enough.

**Advantages:** a much lower monthly cost than a string of short stays, a real home, and a **lease that works as proof of address** for a residence card, a bank or your children's school. Our guide [Moving to Marrakech](/en/blog/moving-to-marrakech-expat-guide) covers these steps.

**Worth knowing:** the commitment is one year. If your plans are still uncertain, start with a short stay to visit the neighbourhoods.

## Short-term rental: try before you commit

A stay of a few days to a few weeks lets you discover the neighbourhoods, check the climate and the pace of life, and view long-term rentals in person. It is also the right choice for a holiday or a business trip.

**Advantages:** no heavy paperwork, a ready-to-live-in home, and flexible dates.

**Worth knowing:** beyond a few weeks, the total cost generally exceeds a yearly rent for a comparable property. Also check what is included (cleaning, linen, electricity, internet): that is often where price differences hide.

## Furnished or unfurnished: a separate question

Whatever the duration, furnished versus unfurnished affects both budget and comfort.

- **Furnished:** you arrive with your suitcases. Ideal for a first year, or if you do not plan to stay long.
- **Unfurnished:** you set it up your way, with a lower deposit (one month). Better suited to a lasting move.

During viewings, check the air conditioning **and** the heating, the state of the pool or garden if there is one, and the planned upkeep: in Marrakech, a home that feels great in May may not in August.

## How to choose

1. **Under 2 months:** short-term.
2. **Between 2 months and a year:** compare the total cost. A one-year lease often stays cheaper, even if you travel for a few weeks a year.
3. **A year or more, or you want to settle:** long-term.
4. **Unsure about settling:** a short stay first, then long-term once you have picked a neighbourhood. Our guide [Where to live in Marrakech](/en/blog/best-areas-to-live-marrakech) can help.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum lease length in Marrakech?

At Live In Marrakech, long-term rentals are on a written lease of one year minimum. Shorter stays are short-term rentals, with different conditions.

### How much is the security deposit?

The deposit is two months' rent for a furnished home and one month for an unfurnished one.

### What documents do I need to rent?

A passport or national ID card is enough to rent with Live In Marrakech.

### Is renting by the year cheaper?

Beyond a few weeks, a yearly rental generally costs less than a series of short stays for a comparable property. Compare the total cost, charges included.

### Can the lease be used as proof of address?

Yes, a written lease serves as proof of address for many procedures: residence card, opening a bank account, school enrolment. Check the documents each organisation requires.

### Can I view properties before renting for a year?

Yes. We arrange viewings in person or by video. A short stay is also a good way to discover the neighbourhoods before signing.
