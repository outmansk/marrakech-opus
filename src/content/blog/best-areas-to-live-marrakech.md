**In short:** to live centrally and do everything on foot, choose **Gueliz**; for calm and an upmarket setting, **Hivernage**; for historic charm, the **Medina**; for space and a garden, the **Palmeraie**, the **Fez road** or the **Ourika road**; for a recent residence with a pool on an often gentler budget, **Agdal**, **Targa** or **Chrifia**.

## Gueliz: the modern city

Built during the French protectorate, Gueliz is Marrakech’s modern centre: shops, cafés, restaurants, galleries, gyms and doctors. Most people live in apartments, in recent buildings or residences from the 1990s and 2000s.

**For:** professionals, couples and expats who want to do everything on foot and manage without a car day to day. **Good to know:** it is lively, so sometimes noisy near the main roads.

## Hivernage: the upmarket residential area

Between Gueliz and the Medina walls, Hivernage gathers large hotels, restaurants and high-end residences. Streets are wide and calmer than in Gueliz, with a few villas.

**For:** those looking for an upmarket, quiet setting minutes from the centre. **Good to know:** properties here are among the most sought after in the city.

## The Medina: living in a riad

The old town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is the realm of riads: houses built around a courtyard, with a roof terrace. Life is on foot, among the souks and monuments.

**For:** heritage lovers, artists, guesthouse projects. **Good to know:** narrow alleys, limited car access, and a legal status to check closely ([see our guide](/en/blog/land-title-melkia-off-plan-morocco)). Our [riads for sale](/en/for-sale/riads-marrakech).

## The Palmeraie: space and greenery

North-east of the city, the Palmeraie mixes large villas, gated estates, golf courses and upmarket residences among the palm trees. The centre is a drive away.

**For:** families and anyone who wants a big garden and a private pool. **Good to know:** a car is essential. See our [villas for sale](/en/for-sale/villas-marrakech) and [villas for long-term rent](/en/long-term-rental/villas-marrakech).

## Agdal, Targa and Chrifia: recent residential areas

**Agdal**, south of the Medina, has grown around recent residences with pools and parking, shopping centres and hotels. **Targa**, to the west, is a quiet residential area of villas and apartments. **Chrifia**, to the south-west, offers recent homes at often more affordable prices, a little further from the centre.

**For:** families and professionals who want a secure residence with a pool without Hivernage prices. See our [apartments for long-term rent](/en/long-term-rental/apartments-marrakech).

## The Fez, Ourika and Amizmiz roads: countryside on the city’s doorstep

These roads leading out of Marrakech are lined with villas on large plots, often with Atlas views to the south (Ourika, Amizmiz). You gain space and quiet, at the cost of longer trips.

**For:** remote workers, families, building projects. **Good to know:** check access, water supply and the plot’s legal status. See our [land for sale](/en/for-sale/land-marrakech).

## How to choose?

Ask yourself three questions: **do I need a car?** (no in Gueliz and Hivernage, yes elsewhere), **do I need a garden or a private pool?** (outskirts) and **which trips will I make every day?** (school, work, airport).
